About Stackly

OUR STORY

How It All Began

Stackly was born out of a simple idea: that everyone deserves a beautiful home that reflects their personality, without compromising on quality or sustainability. What started as a small boutique in New York has now grown into a globally recognized home decor brand.

Our founder, Sarah Jenkins, spent years traveling the world, seeking out master artisans and sustainable materials. Today, we bring those curated, timeless pieces directly to your doorstep, making premium home decor accessible to everyone.
